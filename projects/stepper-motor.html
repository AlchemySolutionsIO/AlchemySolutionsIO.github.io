Project Overview

The client had a working stepper motor setup that wasn't good enough: motion was imprecise, unstable under load, and slow to respond to control inputs. Rather than replacing hardware, we re-engineered the control firmware — often the highest-leverage fix in motion systems.

The upgraded system drives a NEMA-class stepper through a dedicated driver stage, with an LCD panel and rotary control for direct hands-on operation — no PC required at the machine.

What We Improved

  • Precision — refined step control for accurate, repeatable positioning
  • Stability — consistent motion under load, without stalls or lost steps
  • Responsiveness — control inputs translate to motion without lag
  • Operator interface — LCD display with direct manual control at the machine
