Jet-Boat Deflector & Throttle Control System

A joystick-driven control system for a jet boat, built for a repeat client: independent port and starboard deflector control through heavy-duty linear actuators, custom calibration curves, and servo-coupled engine throttle. Watch the actuator prototype running below.

Project Overview

On a jet boat, the deflectors are the steering and the reverse — they redirect the jet's thrust, and how precisely they respond decides how the boat handles. Our client needed the port and starboard deflector controllers re-engineered: joystick inputs mapped to actuator movement through custom calibration curves, so full-ahead to full-astern happens in exactly the travel the helmsman expects.

The system reads joystick positions on a 0–255 scale with a 128 center point, drives each deflector's heavy-duty linear actuator through a relay power stage, and constrains deflector motion to a calibrated band. Beyond the deflector limits, each side's servo-driven throttle takes over — pushing the control further increases engine revs, giving smooth, single-hand control of direction and power together.

What We Built

  • Independent port & starboard deflector control — each side driven by its own actuator and control channel
  • Custom calibration curves — joystick travel remapped so the full maneuvering range sits exactly where the operator wants it
  • Servo-coupled engine throttle — per-side engine speed control that engages progressively beyond deflector limits
  • High-load relay switching — actuator currents kept isolated from the controller logic
  • Iterative delivery with the client — calibration refined across multiple rounds of on-boat feedback
