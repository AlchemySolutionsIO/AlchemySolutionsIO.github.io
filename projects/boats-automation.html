Project Overview

Boats still rely heavily on manual mechanical operation. This prototype demonstrates how on-board mechanisms can be automated with electric linear actuators and an embedded controller — bringing push-button operation to hardware that traditionally needs muscle.

The system drives two heavy-duty linear actuators through a relay switching bank, controlled by an Arduino Mega. The control logic manages extension, retraction, and synchronized movement of both actuators, with the switching architecture sized for real actuator loads rather than bench toys.

What the Demo Shows

  • Dual heavy-duty linear actuators under embedded control
  • Relay-bank power switching, isolated from the controller logic
  • Synchronized extend / retract motion sequences
  • Complete working prototype — not a simulation

From Prototype to Product

This is exactly how we approach product development: prove the mechanism with a working prototype first, then harden it — custom PCB to replace the breadboards, marine-grade enclosures and wiring, and wireless control from a helm panel or phone app. If you're building automation for marine or any harsh environment, this is a conversation worth having.
